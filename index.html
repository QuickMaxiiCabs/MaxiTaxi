Core Service & Booking Policies

Quick Maxii Cabs provides accessible maxi taxi and NDIS transport services across Melbourne. All bookings are subject to availability, completion of accurate trip details, and adherence to our vehicle capacity and passenger support requirements.

Safety & Equipment Standards

Our fleet is maintained to high safety standards and equipped for wheelchair access where required. Drivers are trained in handling mobility equipment and passenger care, and all trips follow strict vehicle hygiene and safety checks.

Financials & Subsidies

Pricing is provided at the time of booking and may be adjusted for distance, vehicle type, and special assistance needs. NDIS participants should confirm subsidy eligibility and funding arrangements before travel; any payment gap is the passenger’s responsibility.
